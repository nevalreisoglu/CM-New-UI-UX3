Policies

Policies are objects attached to offers, journeys or groups of them — not global parameters. Existing communication rules (consent, overlap, cooldown, pre-sent) keep running underneath at delivery time.

Evaluation order

1. Entry
Invitation policy — is the customer inside a quiet period for this offer group?
2. Step
Communication policy — may this outbound go out today, on this channel?
3. Channel
Per-channel control group and region switch.
4. Delivery
Existing ECM rules: consent, overlap, cooldown, pre-sent.
5. Priority
Customer-level priority rules with a time window, applied before the decision API answers.
An invitation policy removes the customer from the journey; a communication policy only skips the send and keeps the journey state.

Segments

Segments are built with the query builder on a DataMart (Main or Event/feedback) and belong to a Segment Group. A segment is a campaign's target audience, or an exclusion list that removes customers from deliveries on the chosen channels.
